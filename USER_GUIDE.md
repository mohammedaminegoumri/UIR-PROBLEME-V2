# UIR PROBLEMES - User Guide

Welcome to UIR PROBLEMES! This guide will help you get the most out of the platform and understand how to use each feature.

## Getting Started

### First Visit

When you first visit UIR PROBLEMES, you'll see a consent banner at the bottom of the page:

1. **Read the Privacy Policy**: Click "Read our Privacy Policy" to understand how we protect your data
2. **Accept or Decline**: 
   - **Accept**: Allows the platform to use local storage for saving your content
   - **Decline**: You can browse but won't be able to submit content

**Important**: All your data stays on your device. We never send it to any server.

### Navigation

The main navigation menu includes:
- **Home**: Overview and quick access to features
- **Submit Problem**: Share your challenges anonymously
- **Crush Finder**: Look for that special someone on campus
- **Forum**: Organized discussions by topic
- **Anonymous Chat**: Real-time anonymous conversations

## Features Guide

### 1. Submit Problem

**Purpose**: Get help with academic, campus, personal, or administrative challenges.

**How to Use**:

1. Select a **Category**:
   - Academic: Study help, course questions, exam preparation
   - Campus Life: Events, facilities, student life
   - Personal: Life challenges, advice needed
   - Administrative: Registration, financial aid, procedures
   - Housing: Dormitory, accommodation issues
   - Other: Anything else

2. Write a **Title**: Brief summary (max 100 characters)
   - Example: "Need help with Calculus II final exam"

3. Add **Description**: Detailed explanation (max 1000 characters)
   - Be specific about your problem
   - Include relevant details
   - Ask specific questions if needed

4. Click **Submit Problem**

**Interacting with Problems**:
- **Like**: Click the thumbs up to show support
- **Comment**: Click the comment icon to share advice or experiences
- **Filter**: Use the category dropdown to see specific types of problems

**Example Problem**:
```
Title: Lost my student ID, need help with replacement
Category: Administrative
Description: I lost my student ID card yesterday in the library. 
Does anyone know the procedure to get a replacement? 
Where do I go and what documents do I need?
```

### 2. Crush Finder

**Purpose**: Find and connect with someone you've seen on campus.

**How to Use**:

1. Write a **Title**: Brief description
   - Example: "Looking for the girl from Engineering building"

2. Fill in **Characteristics** (all optional but helpful):
   - **Gender**: Male, Female, or Prefer not to say
   - **Major/Faculty**: Their field of study
   - **Year**: 1st through 5th year or Graduate
   - **Last Seen Location**: Where you saw them
   - **Physical Description**: Height, hair color, style, distinctive features

3. Add **Additional Information**: The story or context
   - Where and when you saw them
   - What they were doing
   - Why you want to connect
   - Any other relevant details

4. **Upload Photo** (Optional):
   - Click the upload area
   - Choose a photo from your device
   - **Important**: Only upload photos you have the right to share
   - Respect privacy - don't upload photos taken without consent

5. Click **Post Crush Search**

**Privacy Guidelines**:
- Don't share personal contact information
- Be respectful in your descriptions
- Don't post inappropriate content
- Respect others' privacy

**Example Post**:
```
Title: Looking for the guy from Computer Science class
Gender: Male
Major: Computer Science
Year: 3rd Year
Location: Building A, Room 203
Appearance: Tall, dark hair, usually wears a blue backpack
Description: We've made eye contact a few times in Data Structures 
class. I'd love to get to know you better. You always sit in the 
third row on the left side.
```

### 3. University Forum

**Purpose**: Organized discussions about university topics.

**How to Use**:

**Creating a New Thread**:

1. Click **New Thread** button
2. Select **Category**:
   - Academic: By major (CS, Engineering, Business, etc.)
   - Student Life: Events, housing, transportation, food
   - Administrative: Registration, financial aid, graduation
   - Year Groups: Connect with your year
   - General Discussion: Off-topic, advice, success stories

3. Select **Subcategory**: More specific topic
4. Write **Thread Title**: Clear, descriptive title
5. Write **Content**: Your post (max 2000 characters)
6. Click **Post Thread**

**Note**: You'll be assigned a random pseudonym (like "SmartStudent742")

**Participating in Discussions**:

1. Browse categories or view all topics
2. Click on a thread to expand and read full content
3. **Like** the thread to show agreement or support
4. **Reply** to add your thoughts:
   - Click the comment icon
   - Write your reply (max 1000 characters)
   - Click Send
   - Each reply gets a new pseudonym

**Forum Etiquette**:
- Stay on topic
- Be respectful and constructive
- Don't spam or post duplicates
- Help others when you can
- Keep discussions relevant to UIR community

**Example Thread**:
```
Category: Academic → Computer Science
Title: Best resources for learning React?
Content: I'm taking Web Development this semester and struggling 
with React concepts. What resources have helped you understand 
components, hooks, and state management? Any recommended tutorials 
or practice projects?
```

### 4. Anonymous Chat

**Purpose**: Real-time anonymous conversations with fellow students.

**How to Use**:

**Joining the Chat**:

1. Click **Anonymous Chat** in navigation
2. Read the chat guidelines
3. Click **Join Chat Room**
4. You'll be assigned a random pseudonym
5. You'll receive a unique color for identification

**Chatting**:

1. Type your message in the input box at the bottom
2. Press Enter or click **Send**
3. Your messages appear in your assigned color
4. Other users see their own colors

**Chat Features**:
- **Online Users**: See who's currently in the chat (sidebar)
- **Your Identity**: Your pseudonym is shown at the top
- **Message History**: See recent messages
- **Leave Chat**: Click "Leave Chat" when done

**Important Notes**:
- Pseudonyms change each session (they don't persist)
- Chat history is local to your device
- Be respectful and follow guidelines
- Don't share personal information

**Chat Guidelines**:
- Be respectful to all participants
- No personal information sharing
- Keep discussions appropriate
- No harassment or bullying
- Stay on topic when possible

### 5. Privacy & Your Data

**Understanding Data Storage**:

All your data is stored **only on your device** using browser local storage:
- Your submitted problems
- Your crush finder posts
- Your forum threads and replies
- Your chat history
- Your consent preferences

**What This Means**:
- ✓ Complete privacy - data never leaves your device
- ✓ No servers can be hacked
- ✓ You have full control
- ✗ Data doesn't sync across devices
- ✗ Clearing browser data removes everything

**Managing Your Data**:

**To delete all data**:
1. Clear your browser's data/cache
2. In Chrome: Settings → Privacy → Clear browsing data → Select "Cookies and other site data"

**To delete specific content**:
- Future feature: Edit/delete buttons on your posts

**To export your data**:
- Open browser console (F12)
- Type: `localStorage`
- Your data is stored in JSON format

## Troubleshooting

### Can't Submit Content
- Check if you accepted consent
- Ensure browser allows local storage
- Try refreshing the page
- Check if you're using a supported browser

### Content Disappeared
- Did you clear browser data?
- Are you using the same device/browser?
- Check if you're in private/incognito mode

### Photos Not Uploading
- Check file size (keep under 1MB)
- Ensure it's an image file (JPG, PNG)
- Try a different photo
- Check browser storage isn't full

### Chat Not Working
- Ensure you've joined the chat room
- Check internet connection
- Refresh the page
- Try leaving and rejoining

## Safety & Privacy Tips

### Protecting Your Identity
- Never share personal contact information
- Don't mention identifying details
- Don't use patterns in your posts
- Remember: it's anonymous for a reason

### Staying Safe
- Don't arrange to meet people from the platform
- Don't share financial information
- Don't click suspicious links
- Report concerning behavior (feature coming soon)

### What to Avoid Sharing
- Full name, email, phone number
- Student ID number
- Home address
- Class schedule specifics
- Photos with identifiable information
